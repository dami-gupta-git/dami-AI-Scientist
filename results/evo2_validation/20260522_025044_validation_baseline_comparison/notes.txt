# SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

## Main Result: Evo2 Does NOT Generalize to Arbitrary Functional Classifications

**Direct Comparison to DNA Repair vs TSG Baseline:**
- DNA repair vs TSG: **0.78 AUROC** (baseline)
- Glycolysis vs T-cell signaling: **0.65 AUROC** (best across all experiments)
- **Difference: -0.13 AUROC** (17% relative decrease)

## Evidence Supporting Non-Generalization:

### 1. Lack of Geometric Separation (Run 1)
- **Centroid cosine similarity: 0.9986** - Class centroids are nearly identical
- **Centroid cosine distance: 0.0014** - Essentially no separation in embedding space
- **Within-class similarities: ~0.95** - High cohesion within each class
- **Interpretation**: The embeddings do not encode distinguishing features for these functional classes

### 2. Statistical Robustness (Run 2)
- **Mean best AUROC across 5 seeds: 0.665 ± 0.0**
- **Mean final AUROC: 0.510 ± 0.0** (essentially random, severe overfitting)
- **Interpretation**: Poor performance is robust across different train/test splits, not due to chance

### 3. Layer Invariance (Runs 3-4)
- **Layer 14 (early): 0.646 AUROC**
- **Layer 28 (middle): 0.646 AUROC**
- **Layer 40 (late): 0.646 AUROC**
- **All layers: centroid similarity 0.9986**
- **Interpretation**: Lack of functional encoding is consistent across network depth

## Conclusion:

The high performance on DNA repair vs TSG (0.78 AUROC) appears to be **specific to those functional classes** rather than evidence of broad functional class encoding in Evo2. Possible explanations:

1. **Cancer-related sequence features**: DNA repair and TSG genes may have distinctive sequence signatures related to their roles in cancer biology
2. **Evolutionary conservation patterns**: These classes may differ in conservation patterns that Evo2 captures
3. **Sequence composition biases**: Specific to DNA repair/TSG but not generalizable

The glycolysis vs T-cell signaling contrast shows:
- No inherent embedding structure (0.9986 centroid similarity)
- Poor classification performance (0.65 vs 0.78 AUROC)
- Consistency across seeds and layers

**This is a critical negative result**: Evo2 does NOT broadly encode functional class information that generalizes to arbitrary pathway distinctions.
